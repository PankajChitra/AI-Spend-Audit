# AI Prompts

## Purpose

AI was only used for:
- generating personalized summaries

Audit calculations remained deterministic.

---

## Prompt

You are an AI infrastructure cost consultant.

Generate a concise professional audit summary.

Include:
- monthly savings
- annual savings
- optimization opportunities

Tone:
Professional and concise.

Maximum 100 words.

---

## Why This Approach

AI-generated pricing recommendations were inconsistent.

Rule-based calculations were more:
- transparent
- predictable
- testable

AI improved readability only.
